The problem

PE firms manage trillions.
Their ops run on gut and PowerPoint.

The private equity back office hasn't changed in decades. Partners still stitch together portfolio performance from inconsistent Excel submissions, LP reports are built manually every quarter, and deal sourcing relies on relationship networks and stale databases. There is no intelligence layer.

No benchmarking intelligence

Portfolio company KPIs sit in inconsistent formats. Comparing EBITDA margins across 20 portcos takes weeks, not minutes.

Quarterly LP reports take weeks

Associates burn two weeks per quarter assembling LP packages in PowerPoint. Every cycle. Every fund. Every LP.

Deal signals are missed

Regulatory filings, news events, and ownership shifts go undetected. Your CRM doesn't read SEC filings or parse earnings calls.

Value creation plans go untracked

100-day plans live in slide decks. There's no system of record to track initiative progress or hold portcos accountable.

Founder & CEO, Spandan

Sanhik built Spandan from a simple observation: private equity firms making billion-dollar decisions about portfolio companies routinely operate with less data visibility than a mid-size SaaS company has about its own growth metrics.

With deep exposure to PE back-office operations and a background bridging financial services and applied AI, Sanhik is building the intelligence layer the industry has needed for two decades — starting with the four most painful points in the PE workflow: benchmarking, deal sourcing, LP reporting, and value creation tracking.
